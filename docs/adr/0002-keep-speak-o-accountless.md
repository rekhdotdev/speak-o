# Keep Speak-O accountless

Speak-O is an open-source, accountless product: Browser Voice Mode and BYOK Cloud Voice Mode require no Rekh or Speak-O membership, and Speak-O will not operate a paid speech service. BYOK credentials and source text travel directly between the extension and the user-selected Speech Provider rather than through Rekh infrastructure, trading centrally managed billing and provider abstraction for a smaller trust boundary and a product users can operate independently.
